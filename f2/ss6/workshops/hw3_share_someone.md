# HW3 — Share Boss Battle With Someone Special

> Required for everyone · 5 minutes · Before leaving the classroom

---

## Goal

Let someone special (parent, best friend, sibling, grandparent, cousin) play the Boss Battle you built = they see you have built something real.

---

## 4 Steps (in order)

### Step 1 — Open Boss Battle in Trinket

- Go to `trinket.io/python`
- Paste your code from `boss_guess_master.py`
- Press Run to test — play 1 round to make sure it works

### Step 2 — Copy Share Link

- In Trinket, click the **Share** button (top right)
- Choose the **"Link"** tab
- Click **Copy Link** (you get a URL like `trinket.io/python/xxxxxxxxxx`)

### Step 3 — Open LINE chat with someone special

- Open LINE
- Pick a chat with someone special: parent / best friend / sibling / cousin / grandparent — anyone you'd like to play your game
- **Do NOT** post in the class group — send to one person directly

### Step 4 — Paste link + message

Paste the link and type this message:

```
I just passed F2 Survival Challenge!
Want to try the guessing game I built?

(Click the link above — works on mobile too)
```

Send it → **Teacher will check before letting you leave.**

---

## Criteria

- [ ] Link works (the person can open and play)
- [ ] Sent (teacher can see in your LINE)
- [ ] Screenshot the chat — including their reply
- [ ] Show the screenshot to teacher next class

---

## Bonus

- They reply within 30 minutes -> 1 star
- They finish the game (got the right number) -> 2 stars
- They got it in 5 or fewer tries -> 3 stars + Hi-Five from teacher next class!

---

## Who counts as "someone special"

Anyone you'd be proud to share your work with:
- Mom / Dad / step-parent
- Best friend
- Older sibling / cousin
- Grandparent / uncle / aunt
- A teacher you like (from any subject)

**Rules:**
- Send to ONE person (not a group)
- That person should be someone whose reply matters to you
- Avoid sending to classmates (defeats the purpose)

---

## What NOT to share

- Do not include passwords or personal info in your code
- Do not put your friends' real full names in the code
- Do not include personal social media links in the game
